# Collegiate mission and decision matrix — proposed v0.1

Date: 2026-10-24. Baseline: `1745adb`. This is an engineering evaluation proposal, not competition certification or an empirical accuracy claim.

## Source versions and mission implications

The [ESRA documents page](https://www.esrarocket.org/documents-and-forms) links the [2027 R&R REV P1](https://www.esrarocket.org/s/2027-IREC-RR-REV-P1-tcda.pdf) and [2027 DTEG REV P1](https://www.esrarocket.org/s/2027-IREC-DTEG-REV-P1.pdf), both effective October 15, 2026. Source hashes and access date are in the [ledger](sources-and-exposure.json). Treat these as revision P1, rechecking before a real review. Some unrelated page update labels are future-dated; use each PDF's own cover/revision, not those labels.

IREC R&R §3.1.2 (PDF p7) includes 10,000- and 30,000-ft AGL COTS categories, SRAD propulsion categories and two-stage categories. This supports examining a commercial-solid single-stage collegiate mission first; it does not support declaring every collegiate category covered. Target altitude alone does **not** determine peak Mach or angle of attack.

The [NASA 2027 Student Launch handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/g-739882-2027-sli-handbook-508-aug5.pdf), **USLI** vehicle §2.1–2.3 (printed p11; PDF p15), specifies a 4,000–6,000-ft AGL target range and declaration at CDR. The middle/high-school division later in the same PDF has different altitude limits; do not substitute it for USLI.

These are two relevant US collegiate contexts, not a census of college rocketry or evidence of team adoption of Astraea. No team interview or independent team workflow was performed.

## Representative mission records to qualify

These are **selection strata**, not invented as-flown vehicles. Numerical geometry, motor and uncertainty values remain unfilled until evidence exists. The accepted initial direction in `docs/internal/project-status.md` is a single-stage commercial-solid competition vehicle, passive stability and dual-event recovery.

| Stratum | Geometry and motor record | Flight regime to record | Atmosphere and mass properties | Recovery and instrumentation | Decision margin and present coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 — USLI-style full-scale mission | Measured full-scale external geometry, nose/fin profiles, rail-button positions, protuberances; identified commercial solid and exact curve/reload | Actual peak Mach, Re and AoA history; rail crosswind and tip-off; do not assume subsonic from target altitude | Site elevation, pressure definition, temperature, wind aloft and acquisition time; measured launch/dry mass and CG; inertia measurement or bounded estimate | Dual-event triggers/delays, parachute CdA and suspended sections; raw pressure with prelaunch baseline, GPS, synchronized IMU/video where available | Motor/ballast choice versus declared target, rail and recovery limits. Existing small-vehicle apogee evidence does not qualify a full-scale mission |
| M2 — IREC 10k COTS single-stage | Same measurements, including effective guide travel and motor nozzle information for RASAero | Explicitly allocate subsonic, transonic and supersonic cases according to measured/predicted profile; low-AoA aero and rail transient need separate qualification | Site not assumed: DTEG notes 2027 location was unknown at issue. Record altitude-dependent density, speed of sound and wind; burnout CG/mass | Near-apogee initial event, lower main; barometric/GPS altitude with datum and independently identified burnout if phase scoring is required | Altitude targeting, stability through burnout, load envelope and recovery area; no current empirical Astraea claim across these regimes |
| M3 — IREC 30k COTS single-stage | As-flown geometry and surface condition, high-performance motor and nozzle, retention/base geometry | Qualify actual Mach/Re/AoA, pressure and density range; increased altitude is not itself a supersonic proof | Vertical weather structure and extrapolation limits matter; propellant depletion/CG/inertia evolution and motor thrust-reference conditions | Higher ascent/coast, dual recovery and drift; GPS dropout and barometric dynamic-pressure errors explicitly handled | Wider atmosphere/trajectory envelope. Do not imply supported 1.0 until mission-specific evidence exists |
| M4 — subscale or low-energy development flight | Measured scaled geometry and actual motor; similarities and departures from full-scale declared | Record Re/Mach/AoA and mass-ratio mismatch; geometric scaling alone does not preserve dynamics | Same provenance requirements; quantify measurement resolution relative to shorter flight | Instrumented development flight and recovery tests | Useful for workflow, measurement and discriminating diagnostics. It cannot by itself establish full-scale transonic loads or high-altitude dispersion |
| M5 — staged, clustered, SRAD, active-control or other expanded mission | All stage/configuration, ignition and propulsion records required | Separation/plume/interference, ignition transients and control domains | Per-stage mass/inertia, changing reference geometry and uncertainties | Independent sections, triggers, deployment logic and sensors | Research backlog. Not covered by the initial single-stage COTS evaluation; changing its product promise requires coordinator/owner review |

## Decision → output → evidence matrix

“Implemented” below means a contract/source path exists at the pinned baseline. It does not establish a successful production run, numerical accuracy, or reviewer approval.

| Team decision | Astraea output/source at baseline | Measurement or reference needed | Known limits and requirement rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select motor, ballast or geometry for altitude target | `RunSummary.apogee`, `apogeeTime`, `maxMach`; saved scalar run summaries in `src/application/runCases.ts` | Qualified AGL apogee; pressure calibration/datum or GPS uncertainty; actual thrust curve, mass, atmosphere; repeated vehicles and prospective predictions | Apogee validation is presently one small vehicle, as disclosed by `docs/accuracy.md`. A design-change trend needs paired or otherwise controlled evidence; matching one absolute apogee does not verify the ranking of two designs |
| Reach adequate departure speed | `RunSummary.railExitSpeed`; rail configuration in `src/application/workspace.ts` | Effective guide travel, forward/rear button positions, thrust rise, ignition/liftoff timestamps and video/photogates or other qualified speed observation | IREC DTEG §5.3: **25 m/s at first rotational freedom**, not necessarily the final button leaving. Actual ESRA single-stage rail length is listed as 17 ft. NASA USLI §2.14 requires **52 ft/s**. Event equivalence must be audited before comparing numbers; rail tip-off/friction are unsupported in the examined TypeScript manifest |
| Choose fins, CG and stability reserve | `StabilityBreakdown` CP/CG/margin, Mach-dependent aero source; `src/aero/barrowman.ts`, `transonicAero.ts` | Measured launch and burnout CG; CP/CNα against matched wind-tunnel conditions across Mach/AoA; dynamic flight evidence for weathercocking | IREC DTEG §10.2–10.3 uses minimum **7.5% length** subsonic or **10%** if supersonic anywhere, through burnout; max **18% at launch /25% in flight**. Convert using recorded diameter. NASA USLI §2.11 separately requires **2.0 static margin on pad**. A baseline one-caliber boolean cannot establish either rule. CP alone does not validate dynamic stability |
| Size structure and fin stiffness | Telemetry `q`, optional `RunSummary.maxQ`, `FlutterHistory`; `src/aero/finStructure.ts` bending/deflection estimates | Density and air-relative speed, AoA/CN/loads, material properties and joint stiffness; matched coefficient data plus structural/ground tests | Contract describes max-Q as the maximum of **bounded display samples**, not a continuous maximum. DTEG §8.2 requires structural integrity and a reported flutter calculation. There is no source-backed universal percent-error allowance here. Fin model uses a clamped equivalent beam; root attachment, composite behavior and flutter require separate qualification |
| Set recovery events and descent conditions | Event list; `landingSpeed`, `landingEnergy`, `flightTime`; empirical canopy drag | Actual trigger logic, sensor latency, inflation/opening sequence, CdA, suspended mass of each section, deployment and landing observations | IREC DTEG §6.3–6.4: initial event near apogee; drogue **20–40 m/s at main**, main at **≤457 m AGL**, landing **<11 m/s**. NASA USLI §3: main **≥500 ft**, apogee delay **≤2 s**, **≤75 ft·lbf per independent section**, descent **≤100 s**. These vehicle limits are not accuracy tolerances. Inflation/opening shock and terrain-relative deployment are unsupported in the examined manifest |
| Select launch conditions and plan recovery footprint | `Dispersion` landing points/covariance/r50/r90/r99 and run manifest | Wind direction/speed aloft with timestamp, bias and forecast uncertainty; GPS landing of each section, terrain and recovery boundary | NASA USLI §3.10 limits recovery to **2,500 ft radius**. IREC §6.2 makes recovery-area escape a range-safety issue. A covariance ellipse or radius from an input-only ensemble does not establish real containment. Tail calibration, failed runs, wind correlations and usable footprint width must be evaluated |

Rule summaries above are deliberately concise; the cited clauses control. Neither event compliance nor predicted margin authorizes flight: team mentors, competition reviewers and range officials retain their roles. The numerical error budget must be smaller than the team's usable decision reserve after input and measurement uncertainty are accounted for.

## Evidence gaps that determine usefulness

The first qualification record must contain an actual full-scale geometry/motor/Mach/AoA envelope, measured mass/CG and weather, a decision with a stated margin, recoverable source files and measurement limits. Those inputs are not present in this packet. Current local contracts and historical summaries cannot supply them. Prioritize apogee, effective rail speed and through-burnout stability first, while recording q/loads and recovery as separate open evidence gates; do not conceal those gaps behind one overall “validated” flag.
