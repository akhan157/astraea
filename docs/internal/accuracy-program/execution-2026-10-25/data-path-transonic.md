## Search boundary and rejected apparent solutions

The bounded search used 28 targeted web queries, including NASA/NACA terms for finned missiles, small-incidence normal force/pitching moment, M1.2/1.3/1.4/1.5 and one DTIC domain query. The supplement records exact strings. Follow-up downloaded five **public NASA-hosted experimental/method PDFs**, read the relevant methods and inspected actual selected plots; no source was selected based on Astraea residuals. No DTIC data, executable, restricted database or Missile DATCOM access/license was assumed.

Three tempting sources fail this specific gap test:

- [NASA/TM-2005-213541](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20050160470) advertises M.60–4.63, but methods printed p6 say measurements are .60–1.18 and1.70–4.63. The unmeasured interior cannot be supplied by its headline range or an attached CD-ROM claim. Primary PDF GET200 and method text checked.
- [NACA RM A52C04](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19930087011) has a representable-looking four-fin 8-cm rocket and headline M.6–1.9. Its **CN/Cm sweeps are only M.7 and1.7** (printed pp5–6); M1.2–1.7 data concern zero-incidence axial force. It cannot close a CN/Cm gap. PDF GET200 and full methods checked.
- [NASA TM X-106](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19630004016) covers M.6–1.4 with CN/Cm but concerns **low-fineness blunt cone-cylinder/annular flares**, not a slender planar-finned vehicle. PDF GET200; catalog web fetch403. Excluded without relying on uncertain catalog-rights access or geometric substitution.

## Critical-path decision

Acquire the two selected reports and qualify their exact geometry/conventions first. Digitization then produces two real interior **configuration-specific** checks. Do not interpolate ARCAS CN/Cm across the gap and present it as measurement; do not stitch different shapes into one measured Mach curve. Repeated points on one curve are not independent vehicles or independent source families.

No adequate same-geometry experimental sequence across the full requested interval was established in this bounded search. If a continuous 1.2–1.5 native-aero accuracy claim is required after these development checks, retain a **dedicated measurement dependency**: a documented, exactly representable three/four-aft-fin article, finite relevant Re, controlled transition/roughness, zero deflection and roll, independently measured CN/Cm at preregistered interior Mach stations (e.g.1.25/1.35/1.45) with small positive/negative AoA sweeps, support/base conventions, common moment datum and instrument/repeatability uncertainty. Those stations are a proposed test design, not obtained data or an authorized facility booking. Qualified existing tunnel records with the same fields can substitute. Until that packet exists, keep the continuous gap claim unqualified and state the two available anchor geometries explicitly.
